Case study 01

From delivery noise to product confidence.

A product story is more useful than a responsibility list.

The problem

Release quality and scattered feedback made it harder to see what deserved attention. The job was not simply to deliver more—it was to reduce uncertainty.

Discovery & insight

I would bring together customer friction, support patterns, delivery signals and stakeholder context to identify the smallest high-confidence intervention.

Product decision & trade-off

Prioritize clarity in the end-to-end journey and avoid a broad redesign that would increase delivery risk without resolving the core customer problem.

Collaboration & delivery

Product works as the connective tissue: aligning engineering, QA, architecture, business and customers around an explicit decision and measurable release plan.

Measurement & result

The portfolio metrics report a 40% reduction in post-release defects and a 38% reduction in inbound support inquiries.

What I’d do differently

Create earlier feedback loops around the riskiest assumptions and preserve more qualitative customer context alongside operational metrics.
